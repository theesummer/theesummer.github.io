About

I've spent 11 years building iOS systems at the edges of what the platform can do. Much of that time in R&D environments where figuring it out was part of the job — international teams, no playbook, problems solved from first principles.

"I do my best work when the problem is genuinely hard, the team is close-knit, and what we build actually matters."

I design my own architecture — micro-module MVVM, self-contained feature units that compose like microservices. I maintain a custom networking library evolved over 7 years from URLSession callbacks to Swift structured concurrency. I reach for custom solutions when off-the-shelf creates more problems than it solves.

Open to Senior iOS Engineer and iOS Lead roles. Remote or hybrid preferred.

Beyond engineering — VANNA

I tried to build VANNA — a marketplace for traditional Andhra handicraft artisans so they could earn sustainable income without abandoning their craft.
